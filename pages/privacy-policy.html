Data Governance

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ROCare protects, structures, and manages customer and purifier details to deliver safe water solutions.

Policy Sections

  • 1. Data Collection
  • 2. Data Usage
  • 3. Information Sharing
  • 4. Security Measures
  • 5. Customer Rights

1. What Data We Collect

To schedule technician dispatches, track active warranties, and provide annual maintenance contract support, ROCare must collect specific items of customer information.

We collect your name, phone number, email address, and physical service location. This details are strictly used to coordinate appointments, send reminders, and complete on-site services.

Our database logs purifier model types, installation timestamps, diagnostic readings (incoming/outgoing TDS levels), and filter change logs. This helps our team bring matching replacement kits to visits.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We process information to ensure your drinking water stays pure and that customer service queries are completed promptly.

We process location and contact details to manage technician dispatches, calculate travel parameters, and verify plan coverage eligibility under active AMC contracts.

Diagnostic history (filter install dates, water usage, diagnostic notes) is processed to calculate when filters are approaching exhaustion. This updates your customer dashboard reminder cards automatically.

3. Information Disclosures & Sharing

ROCare does not sell or share customer contact databases with third-party marketing companies. Sharing is restricted to core service providers.

Dispatched on-site technicians are given access to the client name, phone number, address, and purifier symptoms checklist only for the duration of the scheduled visit.

When third-party components or specialized pumps are replaced, part serial numbers and basic purifier models may be synchronized with manufacturers for warranty registration.

4. Data Encryption & Security Measures

We implement technical security policies to shield your address registry, payment transaction logs, and credentials from breaches.

Account credentials and diagnostic data are encrypted during transit (HTTPS) and stored on secure cloud database instances. Access credentials are limited strictly to administrative roles.

Cookies are used to manage session logins, remember UI theme parameters (light/dark preference), and store direction toggles (LTR/RTL). We do not run persistent cross-site tracking pixels.

5. Customer Rights & Preferences

You maintain rights over your information, including accessing details, correcting address data, or opting out of diagnostics logs.

You can request a report of all purifier replacements, AMC invoice logs, and address histories recorded. Profile corrections can be updated instantly from customer dashboards.

Upon cancellation of AMC plans, you can ask for complete profile deletion. Our systems will wipe contact profiles and diagnostic logs within 30 days, retaining only legally mandatory tax/invoice records.
