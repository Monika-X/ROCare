Alkaline: Hype or Health?

What pH 7.4 water actually does for digestion and taste — beyond marketing claims.

August 2026 4 min read
At a Glance

pH 7.4 — the sweet spot

Not too acidic, not too alkaline — just right for taste and digestion.

7.4blood pH ideal
7–7.5ideal drinking pH
5.5RO without remineralizer

What pH means

pH 7 is neutral. Below 7 acidic, above alkaline. RO without remineralizer gives 5.5–6, slightly acidic. A remineralizer adds calcium/magnesium to bring to 7–7.5 — crisp taste, better hydration.

Beyond hype

pH 8–9.5 marketed alkaline isn't a cure. But ensuring 7–7.5 is science-backed for taste and digestion.

Digestion & taste

At 7.4, water tastes sweeter, supports enzymes, and avoids enamel irritation from acidic RO. Simple remineralizer fixes it on next service.
