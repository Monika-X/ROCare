3. Service Bookings & Technician Visits

Diagnostic visits, repairs, and installations are scheduled using our online booking wizard or support agents. Standard diagnostic fees are quoted prior to dispatch and cover the visit, physical inspection, and digital water analysis.

The customer must provide safe, clean, and unobstructed access to the water purifier system. Our technicians reserve the right to refuse service if conditions present an electrical hazard or unsafe environment.

Cancellations or slot reschedules must be completed at least 4 hours before the booked visit window. Late cancellations or technician lock-outs may result in a standard travel fee charge on your next invoice.

4. Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

AMC plans are tailored to guarantee clean water output and cover scheduled maintenance intervals. The details of parts included (sediment filters, carbon pre-filters, RO membranes) vary depending on the chosen tier (Standard, Premium, or Platinum).

Sediment and carbon pre-filters are replaced on a fixed 6-month or 12-month schedule. Additional requests for early replacements due to high incoming TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) levels may incur parts charges if exceeding the plan's allocation.

RO Membrane and booster pump replacement is only covered under Premium and Platinum tiers. Standard AMC plans cover labor and routine filtration kits only. Diagnostic readings are recorded inside the customer dashboard.

5. Parts Warranty & Liability

Replacement spare parts (valves, pumps, power supplies, filter nodes) carry a limited ROCare warranty. The warranty applies solely to factory defects and does not cover damage caused by power fluctuations, municipal water supply outages, or customer modifications.

ROCare's total liability for service issues, technician delays, or filtration performance concerns is capped at the invoice amount paid by the customer for that specific visit or active AMC year.
