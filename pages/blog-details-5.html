UV vs. UF Sterilization

How ultraviolet reactors and ultrafiltration capillaries guard municipal tap water from dangerous microbes.

August 2026 6 min read
At a Glance

Two technologies, one goal: microbe-free water

UV and UF work differently but complement each other in protecting your family from waterborne pathogens.

0.01μmUF membrane pore size

How UV Sterilization Works

UV sterilization uses a UV-C lamp emitting at 254 nanometres — a wavelength that penetrates bacterial and viral DNA, disrupting their replication mechanism. Microbes exposed to sufficient UV-C dosage become non-viable without being physically filtered out. This means dead bacteria and viruses remain in the water as harmless particulate matter that is safe to consume. UV is highly effective against bacteria (E. coli, Salmonella), viruses (Hepatitis A, Rotavirus), and protozoa (Giardia, Cryptosporidium).

Key Insight

UV does not remove dissolved chemicals, heavy metals, or TDS. It only deactivates biological threats. This is why UV is almost always used as a secondary stage after the RO filtration membrane.

How Ultrafiltration (UF) Works

UF uses hollow-fibre membranes with pores of 0.01–0.1 microns. These pores physically block bacteria, viruses (partially), cysts, and colloids as water passes under gentle gravity or low pressure. Unlike RO, UF does not reject dissolved minerals, making it suitable for areas where source water TDS is already acceptable (below 200 ppm) but microbe risk is high. UF operates without electricity — a significant advantage in power-uncertain areas of India.

Which is Better?

Neither is universally better — they serve complementary roles. A full-spectrum system combines RO (mineral and chemical removal) + UV (biological sterilization) + UF (additional microbial barrier). The right choice depends on your source water: high TDS municipal or borewell water needs RO+UV, while low-TDS supply water in hill towns may need only UF. ROCare technicians assess your water source before recommending a configuration.

Key Takeaways

Choose the right protection layer

UV for biological threats

UV-C effectively kills 99.99% of bacteria and viruses without adding any chemicals to your water supply.

UF for physical removal

UF physically removes bacteria and cysts at 0.01 micron — works without electricity and preserves healthy minerals.

Combine for best results

RO + UV + UF together provides chemical, biological, and physical protection — the gold standard for Indian households.
